# Selection and sources

Checked 10 November 2026. Tallyfy is the incumbent; recurring procedure execution is the family. Procedure versions become runs with ordered tasks, evidence, blocks and handovers. This differs from a procedure library's review governance and a request-approval queue.

US$15,000 a year is a sizing scenario for 50 Full Seats at the vendor's US$300 annual rate. It is not an observed customer invoice. [Tallyfy pricing](https://tallyfy.com/pricing/) also lists Light Seats and a separate analytics feed. The page uses published unit prices only, without multiplying a hypothetical team into a claimed bill.

Scores, out of five: annual bill 2, data shape 5, buyer fit 2, search demand 2, one-session buildability 5, import path 4. Total 20/30. Recurring review packs are relevant to owner-led professional firms and operations teams. We did not verify a Tallyfy customer or invoice in the live CRM. Search results establish alternatives interest, not measured search volume.

Compared candidates: Process Street scored 19/30, with quote-only current pricing and a documented export. Manifestly scored 19/30; its published Business annual rate is lower and a larger Enterprise quote was not verified. Orderful was excluded because trading-network connectivity is central to its offer.
