# Procedure evidence controls

This desk checks recorded evidence. It does not certify legal compliance, authenticate the named operator or decide the lawful retention period. Sources checked 10 November 2026.

| Rule | Check | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| RETENTION-01 | A local run's recorded retention review date has passed | [NZ Privacy Act principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) requires personal information to be kept only as long as its lawful purpose requires. The date is set by the operator's policy, not a statutory period generated by this software. |
| ACCESS-02 | A procedure has no recorded access safeguards evidence | [NZ Privacy Act principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) requires reasonable safeguards. A text reference is a review prompt, not proof that technical controls exist. |
| IMPORT-05 | An imported batch lacks a purpose, retention review date or access evidence, or its review date has passed | The same [retention principle](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) and [security principle](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) apply to imported personal records. Review the source, access and purpose before use. |

## Internal controls

REVIEW-03 flags an overdue approved-procedure review. WAIVER-04 lists locally waived steps. These are internal operating controls, not laws. Owner and reviewer must have different recorded names. The system does not verify those identities. Each completed or waived step requires an evidence reference, and all earlier steps must be resolved first. Blocked steps must be released with evidence. Old approved versions and decision events cannot be updated through the database's normal write operations; new records preserve changes.

No legal retention period is hard-coded. The demonstration's dates, seven-day inactivity prompt and step deadlines are examples of business policy. Configure your own policy and obtain professional advice where your records have sector-specific duties. The desk records that work; it does not replace it.
